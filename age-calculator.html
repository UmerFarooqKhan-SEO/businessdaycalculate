How Does the Age Calculator Work?

Enter your date of birth and choose the date you want to calculate your age as of. The calculator then works out your exact age using calendar years, months and days.

It also calculates the total number of completed months, elapsed calendar days and approximate weeks between the two dates.

What Does Exact Age Mean?

Exact age describes how long you have lived using years, months and days rather than simply counting the number of years.

For example, someone may be 26 years, 7 months and 30 days old on a particular date. This provides more detail than simply saying they are 26 years old.

How Is Age Calculated?

The calculator compares your date of birth with the selected calculation date. It calculates completed years first, followed by the remaining months and days.

Total days are calculated from the actual calendar difference between the two dates, including leap years.

What Is the Next Birthday?

Your next birthday is the next occurrence of your birth date after the selected calculation date. The calculator also shows how many calendar days remain until that date.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the age calculator work?

Enter your date of birth and the date you want to calculate your age as of. The calculator calculates your completed years, months and days.

Can I calculate my age on a past date?

Yes. Select any date after your date of birth in the "Calculate Age As Of" field.

Does the calculator show my next birthday?

Yes. It shows your next birthday and the number of days remaining until it occurs.

Can I calculate my age in total days?

Yes. The result includes the total number of elapsed calendar days between your date of birth and the selected date.
