What is a business day?

A business day is normally a weekday when businesses are open and work is carried out. In many countries, Monday through Friday are treated as business days, while Saturday and Sunday are excluded.

How does the Business Day Calculator work?

Enter a start date and an end date, choose your working week, and optionally enter holidays. The calculator counts the selected working days in the date range and excludes the dates you specify as holidays.

Business days vs calendar days

Calendar days include every day in a date range, including weekends and holidays. Business days only count the days that match your selected working schedule, making them useful for deadlines, delivery estimates, contracts and project planning.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the calculator exclude weekends?

Yes. By default, Saturday and Sunday are excluded. You can choose Monday to Saturday or create a custom working week.

Can I exclude public holidays?

Yes. Enter holiday dates in the optional holiday box, with one date per line.

Can I include the start and end dates?

Yes. You can independently include or exclude both the start date and end date.
