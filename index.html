What is a business day?

A business day is generally a day when businesses and organisations operate. In many countries, Monday through Friday are considered business days, while Saturday and Sunday are treated as weekends. However, working schedules can vary between businesses, industries and countries.

How does the Business Day Calculator work?

Enter your start date and end date, select your working week and optionally enter holidays. The calculator checks each date in the selected range and counts the days that match your chosen working schedule.

You can also choose whether to include the start date and end date. This makes the calculator useful for project planning, deadlines, delivery estimates, contracts, payroll calculations and other date-based tasks.

Business days vs calendar days

Calendar days include every day in a date range, including weekdays, weekends and holidays. Business days only count dates that fall within your selected working schedule.

For example, a seven-day calendar period can contain fewer business days when weekends are excluded. Adding public holidays can reduce the number further.

Why calculate business days?

Working-day calculations are useful when a deadline depends on business operations rather than ordinary calendar days. Businesses and individuals can use them for project schedules, delivery estimates, invoices, contracts, service deadlines and planning.

Frequently Asked Questions

Yes. By default, Saturday and Sunday are excluded. You can also select Monday to Saturday or create a custom working week.

Can I exclude public holidays?

Yes. Enter holiday dates in the optional holiday box, with one date per line.

Can I include the start and end dates?

Yes. You can independently include or exclude both the start date and end date.

Is this Business Day Calculator free?

What is the difference between business days and calendar days?

Calendar days include every day in the date range. Business days only include dates that match your selected working schedule.

Can I use a custom working week?

Yes. Select Custom and choose the individual days that should be treated as working days.
