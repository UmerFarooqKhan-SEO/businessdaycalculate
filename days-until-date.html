What Is a Days Until Date Calculator?

A days until date calculator tells you how many days remain between today and a specific future date. It can be used for birthdays, holidays, vacations, deadlines, appointments, exams, events, and other important dates.

How Does the Calculator Work?

The calculator compares the starting date with your target date and calculates the exact number of calendar days between them.

Today's date is selected automatically, but you can change the starting date if you want to calculate the number of days from another date.

Examples of When to Use It

  • Find out how many days until your birthday.
  • Calculate how many days remain until Christmas.
  • Count down to a vacation or trip.
  • Find the number of days until a project deadline.
  • Calculate the days remaining before an important event.

Days Until vs Days Between Dates

A days until calculator is designed primarily for countdowns to a target date. A days between dates calculator is more useful when you want to compare two dates and see calendar days, inclusive days, business days, or weekends.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many days are left until a date?

Enter your target date and the calculator will compare it with the starting date to show the exact number of calendar days remaining.

Does the calculator use today's date automatically?

Yes. When the page loads, today's date according to your device is automatically selected.

Can I calculate days until my birthday?

Yes. Enter the date of your upcoming birthday as the target date to calculate how many days remain.

Can I calculate days until Christmas?

Yes. Select December 25 as the target date and the calculator will show the number of days remaining.

What happens if the target date has already passed?

If the target date is before the starting date, the calculator will tell you how many days have passed since that date.
