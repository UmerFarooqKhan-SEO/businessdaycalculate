What Is a Subtract Business Days Calculator?

A subtract business days calculator helps you find an earlier date by removing a specific number of working days from a starting date. Unlike a normal date calculator, it does not count weekends as working days.

This can be useful for calculating deadlines, project schedules, delivery dates, payment dates, contract periods and other business-related dates.

How to Subtract Business Days

  1. Choose your starting date.
  2. Enter the number of business days to subtract.
  3. Select your working week.
  4. Add any holidays that should be excluded.
  5. Click Subtract Business Days.

Example

Suppose your starting date is Monday, September 28, 2026 and you need to subtract 10 business days using a Monday-to-Friday working week.

The calculator moves backwards through the calendar and skips Saturday and Sunday. The resulting date is Monday, September 14, 2026.

Business Days vs Calendar Days

Calendar days include every day of the week, including weekends. Business days normally include Monday through Friday, although some organisations operate on Saturdays as well.

This calculator allows you to choose between a Monday-to-Friday and Monday-to-Saturday working week.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a business day?

A business day is normally a weekday when businesses operate. The standard working week is Monday through Friday.

How does the calculator subtract business days?

The calculator moves backwards one calendar day at a time and counts only days that fall within your selected working week. Holidays entered by you are also excluded.

Does it exclude holidays?

Yes. Enter your holiday dates in YYYY-MM-DD format, with one date per line.

Can I use Monday to Saturday?

Yes. Select Monday to Saturday if Saturday is part of your organisation's working week.

What happens if I subtract zero business days?

The result remains the same as the starting date.
