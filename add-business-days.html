What Is an Add Business Days Calculator?

An Add Business Days Calculator helps you find a future date by adding a specific number of working days to a starting date. Weekends are skipped automatically, and you can also exclude holidays.

This can be useful for project deadlines, delivery estimates, payment terms, employee leave calculations, contracts, business planning and other situations where calendar days are different from working days.

How to Add Business Days

Enter your starting date and the number of business days you want to add. The calculator moves forward one day at a time and counts only the days included in your selected working week.

For example, if you add 5 business days to a Monday using a Monday-to-Friday working week, the result will be the following Monday.

Business Days vs Calendar Days

Calendar days include every day of the week, including Saturdays and Sundays. Business days normally refer to working days and exclude weekends.

Public holidays can also affect the result. Because holidays vary between countries, companies and organisations, this calculator lets you enter your own holiday dates.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does adding business days mean?

It means moving forward from a starting date while counting only selected working days and skipping weekends and optional holidays.

Does the calculator exclude weekends?

Yes. The default working week is Monday to Friday. You can also choose Monday to Saturday.

Can I add holidays?

Yes. Enter your holiday dates in YYYY-MM-DD format, with one date on each line.

Does the starting date count?

No. The calculator begins counting business days after the starting date. This makes the tool suitable for common "add X business days" calculations.

Can I add zero business days?

Yes. Adding zero business days returns the starting date.
