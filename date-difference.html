What Is a Date Difference Calculator?

A date difference calculator finds the amount of time between two calendar dates. Instead of showing only the total number of days, this calculator also breaks the difference down into years, months and days.

This makes it useful for calculating ages, contract periods, project durations, anniversaries, subscription periods and other date-based time periods.

How to Calculate the Difference Between Two Dates

  1. Select the starting date.
  2. Select the ending date.
  3. Click Calculate Difference.
  4. View the result in years, months and days.

Example

If the start date is September 14, 2020 and the end date is September 14, 2026, the date difference is:

The calculator also shows the total number of elapsed calendar days and weeks.

Years, Months and Days vs Total Days

A calendar-based date difference is different from simply dividing the total number of days by 365.

Months have different numbers of days, and leap years can contain an additional day. For that reason, this calculator calculates the calendar difference first and separately reports the total number of elapsed days.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate the difference between two dates?

Enter the start date and end date, then click Calculate Difference. The calculator will show the difference in years, months and days.

Can this calculator calculate years, months and days?

Yes. It provides a calendar-based difference in years, months and days.

Does the calculator include the start date?

The date difference represents the elapsed time from the start date to the end date. The total-day result is the standard difference between the two dates.

Can I calculate dates from the past?

Yes. You can use any valid dates, including dates from previous years.
