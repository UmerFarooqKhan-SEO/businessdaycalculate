How Many Days Are Between Two Dates?

The number of days between two dates depends on the dates you choose and whether you count the starting and ending dates. This calculator gives you the standard calendar-day difference as well as an inclusive day count.

It also calculates business days, weekend days and weeks, making it useful for planning deadlines, projects, holidays, contracts and work schedules.

How to Calculate Days Between Dates

  1. Select the starting date.
  2. Select the ending date.
  3. Choose your working week.
  4. Add holidays if you want them excluded from business-day calculations.
  5. Click Calculate Days.

Calendar Days vs Business Days

Calendar days include every day between the two dates, including Saturdays and Sundays.

Business days normally include Monday through Friday. However, some organisations also operate on Saturdays. This calculator lets you choose between Monday-Friday and Monday-Saturday working weeks.

What Does Inclusive Days Mean?

Inclusive days count both the starting date and the ending date.

For example, from September 14 to September 15 there is a difference of 1 calendar day, but there are 2 inclusive calendar days when both dates are counted.

Example

Suppose you want to calculate the number of days between September 14, 2026 and September 28, 2026.

The standard calendar difference is 14 days. If both the starting and ending dates are counted, there are 15 inclusive days.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many days are between two dates?

Enter the two dates into the calculator. It will show the standard calendar-day difference and the inclusive number of days.

Does the calculator count the start date?

The standard calendar difference does not count the starting date as a separate day. The calculator also provides an inclusive count that includes both dates.

Can I calculate business days between dates?

Yes. Select your working week and optionally enter holidays that should be excluded.

Can I calculate weeks between two dates?

Yes. The calculator shows the total number of weeks and remaining calendar days.

Does it include weekends?

Calendar days include weekends. Business-day results exclude non-working weekend days.
