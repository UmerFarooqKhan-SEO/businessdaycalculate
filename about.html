About Business Day Calculate

A simple, free online calculator for working days, business days, weekends and holidays.

What is Business Day Calculate?

Business Day Calculate is a free online tool designed to help people calculate the number of business or working days between two dates. It provides a quick way to determine working days without manually counting weekdays on a calendar.

The calculator can exclude weekends and specific holiday dates. You can also choose different working schedules when your work week does not follow the standard Monday to Friday schedule.

Why was this calculator created?

Counting working days can be useful for many everyday and professional tasks. People may need to calculate business days for project deadlines, delivery estimates, contracts, invoices, employment schedules, applications and general planning.

This tool was created to make that process faster and easier. You enter your dates, select your working schedule and optionally add holidays. The calculator then provides the result directly in your browser.

How does it work?

The calculator checks each date within the selected date range. It determines whether each date belongs to your selected working week and whether it has been entered as a holiday.

By default, the calculator uses a Monday to Friday working week. You can also select Monday to Saturday or create a custom working schedule.

Free to use

Business Day Calculate is free to use. No account or registration is required to calculate working days.

The calculator runs in your web browser, making it convenient to use from a desktop computer, tablet or mobile device.

Important note

Business day definitions can vary between countries, organisations and industries. Public holidays may also differ by location. For this reason, the calculator allows you to enter your own holiday dates and select a working schedule that matches your needs.
