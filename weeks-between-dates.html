How Many Weeks Are Between Two Dates?

To calculate the weeks between two dates, first find the total number of calendar days between the dates. Since one week contains seven days, divide the total days by seven.

This calculator makes the calculation easier by showing both complete weeks and any remaining days.

How the Weeks Calculator Works

Enter a start date and an end date. The calculator calculates the elapsed calendar days and divides them into complete seven-day weeks plus any remaining days.

For example, if two dates are 16 days apart, the result is 2 complete weeks and 2 remaining days.

Weeks vs Calendar Days

Calendar days count individual days, while weeks group those days into periods of seven. This can make longer date ranges easier to understand.

What Is an Inclusive Day Count?

The standard date difference measures elapsed time between the dates. An inclusive count includes both the start date and the end date, so it is one day greater when both dates are valid.
