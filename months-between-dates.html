What Is a Months Between Dates Calculator?

A months between dates calculator helps you determine how many complete calendar months and remaining days exist between two dates.

Unlike calculations that divide the number of days by an average month length, this calculator works with actual calendar months.

How to Calculate Months Between Two Dates

Enter your starting date and ending date, then select Calculate Months. The calculator determines the complete calendar months first and then calculates any remaining days.

For example, from January 15, 2026 to September 15, 2026, there are 8 complete months and 0 remaining days.

Complete Months vs Total Days

Complete months and total days are different ways of measuring the same date range.

  • Complete Months: Full calendar months between the dates.
  • Remaining Days: Days left after complete months are counted.
  • Total Days: Exact number of elapsed calendar days.
  • Inclusive Days: Total days when both the start and end dates are counted.

Why Use This Calculator?

Calculating months manually can become confusing because months have different numbers of days. This calculator provides a simple way to measure calendar periods for planning, contracts, subscriptions, projects, payments, and other date-related tasks.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many months are between two dates?

Enter the two dates and the calculator will show the number of complete calendar months together with any remaining days.

Does this calculator use calendar months?

Yes. It calculates calendar months instead of estimating months by dividing total days by an average number of days.

What are complete months?

Complete months are the full calendar months that fit between the start and end dates.

What are remaining days?

Remaining days are the days left after complete calendar months have been counted.

Does inclusive days include both dates?

Yes. Inclusive days count both the starting date and the ending date.
