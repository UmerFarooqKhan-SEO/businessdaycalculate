What Is a Working Days Calculator?

A working days calculator counts the days that are considered working days between two dates. Unlike a standard calendar-day calculation, it can exclude weekends and public or company holidays.

This makes it useful for project planning, employee schedules, delivery estimates, contracts, payroll calculations and business deadlines.

How to Calculate Working Days

  1. Select your start date.
  2. Select your end date.
  3. Choose your working week.
  4. Add holidays if required.
  5. Click Calculate Working Days.

Working Days vs Calendar Days

Calendar days include every day in a date range, including Saturdays and Sundays. Working days only include the days selected as working days.

For example, with a Monday to Friday working week, Saturday and Sunday are excluded from the working-day count.

Using Holidays

You can enter holidays that should not count as working days. Enter each holiday using the YYYY-MM-DD format, with one date on each line.

For example:

  • 2026-12-25
  • 2026-12-26
  • 2027-01-01

Custom Working Weeks

Not every business follows a Monday to Friday schedule. The custom working week option lets you choose exactly which days should count as working days.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are working days?

Working days are days that count as normal workdays according to your selected working-week schedule.

How does the working days calculator work?

Enter the start and end dates, choose your working week and optionally add holidays. The calculator counts the selected working days in the range.

Can I exclude holidays?

Yes. Enter your holiday dates in YYYY-MM-DD format, one date per line.

Can I use a Monday to Saturday working week?

Yes. Select Monday to Saturday from the Working Week menu.
